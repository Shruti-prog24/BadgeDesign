🏛️ Executive Design Rationale

To move beyond generic fantasy RPG heraldry, this collection reinvents badges as Avant-Garde Horology Talismans. Merging precision Swiss watchmaking mechanics with aerospace hypercar geometry and quantum cryptography, each tier is an original collectible artifact: starting from mechanical copper astrolabe compasses, advancing to solar chronometers and aerodynamic cyber-sigils, and culminating in celestial singularity hyper-cubes.
